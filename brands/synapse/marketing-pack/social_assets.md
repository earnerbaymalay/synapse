## LinkedIn

### Product Launch (Long Form)

**Headline:** One Brain. All Models. — LLM Neurosurgeon Is Here

**Body:**
If you use more than one AI coding tool, you're maintaining the same knowledge in multiple places. Different formats. Different locations. Different drift rates.

LLM Neurosurgeon solves this with a single, canonical, git-backed Brain that every tool reads from.

12 adapters. 4 commands. Zero telemetry.

Scan your machine. Import into the Brain. Project to every tool. Let the auto-sync daemon keep everything in lockstep.

Every sync is a Git commit. Full Time Machine for your AI configuration.

Open source. MIT licensed. Local-first.
