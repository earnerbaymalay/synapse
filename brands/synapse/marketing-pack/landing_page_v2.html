The Problem

The Fragmentation Tax

Every AI tool speaks its own config language. GitHub Copilot wants .github/copilot-instructions.md. Zed expects .zed/agents.md. Cline reads .clinerules.

You end up repeating the same rules across a dozen formats — and they always drift out of sync. That's the fragmentation tax.

7 tools × 5 configs
= 35 places to maintain
One change. Dozens of edits. Every. Single. Time.
The Solution
